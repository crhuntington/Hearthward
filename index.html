Top 10 improvements that build value
Tap any project to learn more. Data sourced from the 2025 Cost vs. Value Report by JLC/Zonda Media.
#1
Garage door replacement
Exterior
~194% ROI
⌄
The highest-ROI project nationally, year after year. A new insulated steel door improves curb appeal, energy efficiency, and security at a relatively low cost. Buyers notice it before they step out of the car — and it photographs beautifully in listings.
#2
Minor kitchen refresh
Kitchen
~113% ROI
⌄
Cabinet refacing, new countertops, and updated hardware return more than full remodels at a fraction of the cost. Surface-level upgrades consistently outperform gut renovations. The kitchen is where buyers make their emotional decision.
#3
Steel entry door replacement
Exterior
~101% ROI
⌄
Your front door is your home's handshake. A new steel door improves security, energy efficiency, and curb appeal with one of the best dollar-for-dollar returns of any single project. Bold colors like navy, black, and red are trending in 2025.
#4
Mid-range bathroom remodel
Bath
~80% ROI
⌄
Bathrooms sell homes. A clean remodel with updated fixtures, a double vanity, walk-in shower, and fresh tile dramatically improves buyer perception and daily livability. Dated bathrooms are one of the most common reasons buyers pass on a home.
#5
Energy-efficient windows
Energy
~68% ROI
⌄
Energy efficiency is the #1 priority for 2025 buyers. New double-pane vinyl windows lower utility bills, reduce drafts, and signal a well-maintained home. They recoup about 68% at resale — and the comfort gains make them a smart investment whether you sell or stay.
#6
Manufactured stone veneer
Exterior
~153% ROI
⌄
Adding stone veneer to the front of your home creates an upscale, architectural look that dramatically improves curb appeal. It's one of the most cost-effective exterior upgrades available and consistently delivers strong returns in competitive markets.
#7
Deck or patio addition
Exterior
~67% ROI
⌄
Outdoor living spaces have been highly valued since 2020. A well-built deck or patio expands usable square footage, improves lifestyle appeal, and gives buyers a reason to pay more. Wood decks average 67% ROI; composite materials cost more but require less maintenance.
#8
Vinyl siding replacement
Exterior
~80% ROI
⌄
Replacing worn or dated siding with new vinyl instantly refreshes the entire exterior and reassures buyers that the home has been well maintained. It also improves insulation and reduces exterior maintenance long-term.
#9
Basement finishing
Interior
~71% ROI
⌄
Finishing an unfinished basement converts unused storage into livable square footage — a home office, entertainment space, or extra bedroom. Every finished square foot adds to appraised value and buyer appeal, especially in markets where buyers are priced out of larger homes.
#10
Fresh exterior paint
Exterior
~55% ROI
⌄
Paint is the cheapest upgrade with the highest visual payoff. A fresh exterior in a neutral, on-trend color makes the entire home feel newer and better maintained. It's the single highest return-per-dollar spent on curb appeal and almost always recommended before listing.
